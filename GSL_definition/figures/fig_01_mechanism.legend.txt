Figure 1. Mechanism at two representative sites (main run: GPP_NT_VUT_REF, negative GPP
set to 0, year screening d80, GSL counts GPP > T).
Low-third site: JP-Fjy, skewness = 0.5123.
High-third site: US-Ton, skewness = 1.2120, group = woody-grass mosaic. Chosen nearest the high third's own 
median skewness, restricted to woody-grass mosaic and grassland sites (drylands), per the
Stage 3 revision -- see docs/methods_memo.md. AU-ASM was excluded from consideration throughout.
Panel a: daily GPP_NT_VUT_REF (negative values set to 0) for each site's median-GSL year (dark line; GSL = days with GPP > threshold at 10% of P99), other screened years in grey, site name and skewness labelled on each row. Dashed lines at 5%, 10% (Z17) and 20% of P99; dotted red line at each site's own T95 (a different linetype, since T95 varies by site).
JP-Fjy T95 = 15.4% of P99.
US-Ton T95 = 12.2% of P99.
Panel b: overlaid daily-GPP density (solid, left axis, % of P99, view limited to 0-100% -- the underlying density estimate itself is not truncated) and mean GSL vs. threshold (dotted, right axis) for both sites. Vertical bracket at 7.5% of P99 shows the change in mean GSL between 5% and 10% of P99 for each site (also stated below).
Change in mean GSL from 5% to 10% of P99 (negative = fewer days qualify at the higher threshold): JP-Fjy = -26.40 days, US-Ton = -34.70 days.
