Figure 2. Where threshold sensitivity sits across all 62 sites (main run: GPP_NT_VUT_REF,
negative GPP set to 0, year screening d80, GSL counts GPP > T).
Panel a: skewness (continuous, capped at 2 -- see docs/methods_memo.md) and functional group (categorical) per site, same row order as panels b/c (ascending skewness bottom to top).
Panel b: heat map of |local sensitivity| (days of GSL lost per 1% of P99, absolute value; darker = more sensitive), 62 sites x log-binned thresholds (0.1-35% of P99, 35 bins). Z17 (black circle) and T95 (red triangle) marked per row -- same convention as panels c and 4c.
Panel c: sensitivity at Z17 (black circle) and at T95 (red triangle), joined by a segment.
Panel d: median local sensitivity per threshold bin (same 35 log-spaced bins as panel b), by skewness third (low/middle/high, boundaries 0.63 and 0.88 per docs/framework.md). Dashed line at 10% of P99 (Z17).
Sites per skewness third: low = 21, middle = 20, high = 21 (total 62).
