GSL_definition/expanded: methods comparison, version 2. Revise code/11_gsl_methods_compare.R in place and regenerate its own tables and draft figures (version 1 stays in git history at 15dc51f). Change nothing else. Decisions since version 1, from Dave and the literature (Yang and Noormets 2021, ESSD; Hu et al. 2010; Eklundh and Jonsson 2017):

1. GPP series. Replace the screened, unscreened and agreement variants with two: NT (zero-floored GPP_NT_REF, the main series, as in Tash's analysis; no winter screen) and DT (zero-floored GPP_DT_REF from the same VUT/CUT family). Apply each method to each series separately, with thresholds from that series' own P99 and fine grid; never mix NT and DT within a series. Report the DT minus NT difference in GSL, start and end per site-year as the partitioning uncertainty, and say where it concentrates (start, end, or winter days).
2. Southern Hemisphere: July to June years for AU-How in every method, including M2 to M4, and for P99. A July to June year passes if it has at most 14 failing days under the existing QC rule. Report how AU-How's years and GSL change against the calendar-year version.
3. M6: search for the start only within 30 days either side of that site-year's M5-span start, and for the end only within 30 days either side of the M5-span end (steepest positive and shallowest negative slope, as before; unsmoothed daily NEP; k = 3 to 9). Drop the alternate end. Site-years without an M5-span get no M6 value and a flag.
4. Two-season flag: replace the slope-run rule with the TIMESAT rule used by Wang et al. (2020), applied to 15-day smoothed GPP: a site-year has two seasons if a secondary peak exceeds 0.5 times the primary peak. Require the series to fall below half the lower peak between them, so that noise near the main peak does not count; document the rule as implemented.

Checks: for Northern Hemisphere sites, M2 at 10% and M4 at 0.95 on the NT series equal the main_unscreened gsl_z16 and gsl_t95 in derived_site_year.csv; every site-year has a value or a flag for every method and series.

Report in docs/gsl_methods_compare_<date>_v2.md: the NT against DT differences by site and method; M6 against version 1 (mean GSL, k-range, agreement with the other methods); the two-season counts by site; the AU-How change; and the three to five largest differences between methods.
