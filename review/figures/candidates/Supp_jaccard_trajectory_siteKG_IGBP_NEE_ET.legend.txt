TITLE: Supplementary Jaccard trajectory — site-level KG, site-metadata
IGBP, and site-measured NEE/ET across four network generations

STATUS: Candidate supplementary figure, addressing a co-author comment on
draft Figs 4/5. Not yet promoted to draft_manuscript_v1/. Companion to
Supp_sampling_ratio_siteKG_IGBP_NEE_ET.png. Full methods in
Supp_methods_siteKG_IGBP_NEE_ET.txt (this directory).

DESCRIPTION:
Single-panel trajectory, directly analogous to draft Fig 5
(review/figures/representativeness/fig_rep008_jaccard_trajectory_with_counts.png),
across the same four network generations (Marconi -> La Thuile ->
FLUXNET2015 -> Current), with the same three axis substitutions as the
companion sampling-ratio figure. Light grey bars (right axis) show
absolute site count per generation.

KEY DIFFERENCE FROM DRAFT FIG 5: the NEE and ET lines connect only 2 of
the 4 points (FLUXNET2015, Current) — La Thuile and Marconi have no
downloaded flux time series anywhere in this repository (only historical
site/coordinate lists), so no measured NEE/ET can be computed for them.
This is an explicit, visible gap in the line (na.rm, not interpolated or
backfilled), documented in the plot's own caption. KG, Aridity, Biomass,
and IGBP all have full 4-point coverage.

AXES AND LINE COLORS:
  KG (13-class, unchanged):              vermilion  #D55E00
  IGBP (17-class, site metadata):        reddish-pink #CC79A7
  Aridity (7-class, unchanged):          amber      #E69F00
  Biomass (7-bin, unchanged):            green      #009E73
  NEE (site-measured NEP magnitude, 18-bin): blue   #0072B2
  ET (site-measured, 18-bin):            sky blue   #56B4E9

Continuous axes (NEE/ET) use 18-bin hybrid here (matching draft Fig 5's
resolution), vs. 7-bin in the companion sampling-ratio figure (matching
draft Fig 4's resolution) — same convention as the original figure pair.
