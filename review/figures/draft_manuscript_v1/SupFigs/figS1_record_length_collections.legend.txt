TITLE: Supplementary Figure S1 — Record length across FLUXNET collections and the snapshot

PUBLICATION LEGEND:
Supplementary Figure S1. For each of four FLUXNET network generations (Marconi, La Thuile,
FLUXNET2015, the snapshot), the share of that collection's sites with at least n years of
data, as a step line. Data used in the analyses are described in Section 2.5.

DESCRIPTION:
Share of each collection's sites with at least n years of data, as a step line. Site counts
shown in the legend key. Collection colours as in Figure 2: Marconi dark grey (#404040),
La Thuile red (#E74C3C), FLUXNET2015 blue (#3498DB); the snapshot is drawn true black, at a
heavier line weight than the other three, so it reads as distinct from Marconi's similarly
dark grey. (Figure 2's own record-length histogram by IGBP class -- formerly this figure's
panel a -- now lives directly in Figure 2's inset; it is not duplicated here.)

IMPORTANT -- two different meanings of 'a year', not interchangeable:
  - Marconi, La Thuile, FLUXNET2015 (the three historical collections): a year counts if that
    site is LISTED as having data for that year in the collection's own published site table
    (La Thuile: 1991-2007 year-indicator columns of data/lists/LaThuileList.xlsx; FLUXNET2015:
    two-digit year columns of data/lists/FLUXNET2015.xlsx, where a plus sign or the text
    'Tier 2' both count as a year with data, same as a plain year marker).
  - The snapshot: a year counts if ANY flux value (of the 12 broad flux variables checked by
    compute_site_year_presence(), R/utils.R) is present in AT LEAST ONE MONTH of that year --
    not a published site-table listing, since the current network has no such table.
  - Marconi values are SPANS ONLY: Marconi_to_Modern_SiteIDs.xlsx records only a first-last year
    range per site (e.g. '1997-1998'), not a year-by-year record, so Marconi's per-site year
    count is that range's length, not a count of individually-confirmed data years. La Thuile and
    FLUXNET2015, by contrast, are genuine year-by-year indicator counts.

Because of this, this figure's four lines are not a strictly like-for-like comparison --
stated here so the figure is not read as claiming otherwise.

COLLECTION TOTALS (sites, site-years): Marconi 35, 96; La Thuile 252, 965; FLUXNET2015 212, 1532; the snapshot 781, 6200.

Final artwork size: 90 mm wide x 90 mm tall, Helvetica throughout. Supplementary Figure (target
journal Scientific Data has no Extended Data concept) -- see docs/figure_inventory.md.
