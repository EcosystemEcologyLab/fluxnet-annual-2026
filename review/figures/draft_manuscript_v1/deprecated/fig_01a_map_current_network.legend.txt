FIGURE LEGEND — fig_03_map_current.png
========================================

TITLE: Global distribution of the current FLUXNET Shuttle network

DESCRIPTION:
Global point map showing the location of every eddy covariance flux tower
site in the current FLUXNET network. Each site is plotted as a single
uniformly-coloured point at its registered latitude/longitude; no
classification, colour-coding, or size-scaling of points is applied — the
figure is a simple presence map. A basemap with country borders (thin,
light grey) and coastlines (thicker, black), light grey land fill, provides
geographic context. No plot title, subtitle, or caption is rendered; site
count is reported here rather than on the figure itself. The only text
drawn on the figure is the bold lower-case panel letter "a" (8 pt
Helvetica, top-left) -- this is panel a of Figure 1 (panel b is
fig_01b_cumulative_siteyears_igbp.png, a separate file; the two are also
combined into one file, fig_01.png, by scripts/generate_fig01_merged.R).

PROJECTION (revised 2026-10-02, task 7):
Equal Earth (EPSG:8857), an equal-area pseudo-cylindrical projection. The
basemap and all site points are cropped to latitude [-56°, 85°] in
lon/lat space BEFORE projecting (excludes Antarctica and the high Arctic;
no real tower falls outside this window). The canvas height is fit to the
map's true projected aspect ratio at this crop (computed by
equal_earth_height_mm() in R/figures/fig_maps.R) rather than forced into a
fixed square — at 89 mm wide this is ~38.5 mm tall, not 89 x 89 mm.

AXES:
No coordinate axes are drawn (map uses a void theme — no tick marks, tick
labels, or axis titles).

POINT STYLING (revised 2026-10-02, task 7):
Shape 16 (filled circle, no separate outline), colour #0072B2 (Okabe-Ito
blue, colour-blind-safe), size 1.0 pt, alpha 0.65 — semi-transparent, so
overlapping towers (dense clusters in Europe and the eastern/western USA)
read as visibly darker rather than simply occluding each other. Colour is
constant — it does not encode IGBP, hub, or any other variable.

NETWORK AND SITE COUNT:
FLUXNET Shuttle network, pinned snapshot: n = 781 sites.
This figure shows the Shuttle network only — it does not overlay or compare
against any historical dataset (Marconi, La Thuile, FLUXNET2015). A separate
companion figure (fig_04_map_snapshots.png, same source script) shows those
three historical networks alongside the current Shuttle network as an
explicitly-labelled four-panel comparison; that figure is not part of this
draft-manuscript set. The four-regional-panel companion
(SupFigs/supp_map_regional.png) outlines North America, Europe, East/
Southeast Asia, and Australia/New Zealand on this same world map and shows
each region close up in its own Lambert Azimuthal Equal-Area projection.

DATA SOURCE:
Site coordinates (location_lat, location_long) and site_id from the FLUXNET
Shuttle snapshot CSV data/snapshots/fluxnet_shuttle_snapshot_20260901T094522.csv
(781 sites), obtained via flux_listall()/flux_download() per the project's
Shuttle-only primary-data policy (CLAUDE.md §1). No FLUXNET2015, AmeriFlux,
or ICOS-FLUXNET data is used in this figure.

EXCLUSIONS:
Sites with missing or out-of-range coordinates (location_lat outside
[-90, 90] or location_long outside [-180, 180], or either value NA) are
dropped before plotting. Duplicate site_id rows are collapsed to one point
per site. No sites were excluded on these grounds in the current snapshot;
all 781 sites are shown.

BASEMAP:
Country polygons and a separate coastline, both from rnaturalearth::
ne_countries()/ne_coastline() (scale = "medium", 1:50m resolution), land
fill grey97 (near-white), s2 spherical geometry disabled for the crop/
render step to avoid outline rendering artefacts. Country-border and
coastline line weights are drawn in two separate layers so the coastline
reads as the dominant silhouette and country borders as a secondary,
lighter reference (task 7).

REPRODUCIBILITY:
Function: fig_map_point_network() in R/figures/fig_maps.R (panel_letter()
  and save_nature_figure() from R/nature_format.R add the panel letter and
  write the PNG/PDF pair; Equal Earth projection and canvas-height-fit-to-
  map revised 2026-10-02)
Script:   scripts/generate_point_maps.R
Output:   review/figures/maps_point/fig_03_map_current.png/.pdf, copied to
          review/figures/candidates/ and then here
DIMENSIONS: 89 mm wide x ~38.5 mm tall (canvas fit to the Equal Earth map,
            not a fixed square), 600 dpi PNG + vector PDF, Helvetica,
            white background.
